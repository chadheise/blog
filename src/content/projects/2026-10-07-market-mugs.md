A web app for collectors of Christmas Market mugs, the souvenir mugs used to serve mulled wine at European holiday markets. Browse a crowd-sourced database of designs, filtered by market, year, location, color, and style, to identify the mugs you own and keep track of your collection.

- Visit [www.marketmugs.com](https://www.marketmugs.com)
- Add mugs you own with your own photos and notes, organized into collections
- Works in any desktop or mobile browser
